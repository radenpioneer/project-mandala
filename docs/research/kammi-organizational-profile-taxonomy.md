# KAMMI organizational profile taxonomy

_Research date: 13 August 2026_

## Scope and confidence

This note records source facts that can inform the participant-profile questions in **Define survey instrument and respondent journey**. It does not choose survey policy or final answer labels.

The strongest substantive source found is the **AD/ART adopted at Muktamar XIII KAMMI in Mataram on 25 May 2024**. The document is an organizational primary document, but the publicly accessible copy is hosted by Scribd rather than an official KAMMI domain. The same provenance caveat applies to the Muktamar XIII National Work Guidelines and the August 2024 secretariat guide draft.

No authoritative, current, publicly accessible master list of PW and PD units was found on the PP KAMMI website. Any geographic taxonomy therefore needs later validation by PP KAMMI or another current organizational registry.

## Verified formal taxonomy

### Organizational structure

The Muktamar XIII AD, Article 12, names five formal management structures:

- Pengurus Pusat (PP)
- Pengurus Wilayah (PW)
- Pengurus Daerah (PD)
- Pengurus Luar Negeri (PLN)
- Pengurus Komisariat (PK)

Each structure is led by its respective chair. This is the best-supported formal taxonomy for a self-declared **management level**. Source: [AD/ART Muktamar XIII, lines 193–206](https://id.scribd.com/document/793543095/Ad-Art-Kammi-Muktamar-Xiii-2024).

The ART further distinguishes internal organs or personnel groupings—Badan Pengurus Harian (BPH), Pengurus Harian (PH), Badan Khusus, Lembaga Semi Otonom (LSO), and Lembaga Otonom (LO)—inside structural levels. These are not additional geographic management levels. See, for example, PW Article 15, PD Article 19, and PK Article 28 in the [same AD/ART copy](https://id.scribd.com/document/793543095/Ad-Art-Kammi-Muktamar-Xiii-2024).

### Membership levels

The Muktamar XIII ART distinguishes two membership categories: **Anggota Biasa** and **Anggota Kehormatan**. Article 3 then defines only three levels within ordinary membership:

- Anggota Biasa I (AB I): passed DM I and AB1 certification
- Anggota Biasa II (AB II): passed DM II
- Anggota Biasa III (AB III): passed DM III

Source: [AD/ART Muktamar XIII, lines 338–370](https://id.scribd.com/document/793543095/Ad-Art-Kammi-Muktamar-Xiii-2024).

No formal AB0, “pra-DM,” or “alumni” membership level appears in these provisions. Anggota Kehormatan is a separate category, not a fourth ordinary-membership level.

### Meaning of registered substructure

ART Article 8 defines member mutation as transfer from one organizational unit to another. It explicitly permits transfer among KAMMI Wilayah, Daerah, and Komisariat, supported by an introduction letter or membership card naming the member's originating structure. A member studying at two universities in different PD work areas must choose one PD. Source: [AD/ART Muktamar XIII, lines 421–433](https://id.scribd.com/document/793543095/Ad-Art-Kammi-Muktamar-Xiii-2024).

This verifies that **the substructure where the participant is registered** is an organizational concept distinct from ordinary domicile. It does not establish how an independent survey should verify the declaration.

### Geography is not a simple administrative lookup

ART Articles 14, 18, 21, and 27 show that organizational geography cannot safely be derived from a standard province/city dataset alone:

- A PW coordinates PD and PLN units and may cover **one or several provinces**.
- A PD may be formed in a central city or a provincial, regency, or city capital that has a university.
- PD units can split. They normally occupy different regency/city areas, but more than one full PD may exist within a central city's administrative area when organizational conditions justify it.
- A PK sits below a PD and may cover one or more universities, one or more faculties, or serve as a city/regency area's pioneering commissariat.

Sources: [PW status and formation, lines 601–612 and 731–741](https://id.scribd.com/document/793543095/Ad-Art-Kammi-Muktamar-Xiii-2024), [PD status and formation, lines 743–756 and 871–923](https://id.scribd.com/document/793543095/Ad-Art-Kammi-Muktamar-Xiii-2024), and [PK status, lines 1077–1093](https://id.scribd.com/document/793543095/Ad-Art-Kammi-Muktamar-Xiii-2024).

### Muktamar XIII expansion plan

The 2024–2026 National Work Guidelines set goals of 34 PW, 150 PD, and 500 PK units, including the formation of 4 new PW and 29 new PD units. Its priority list mentions, among others, PW Gorontalo, PW Bangka Belitung, PW Kaltara, and PW Papua Barat–Barat Daya. These are targets or expansion recommendations, not proof that each structure currently exists. Source: [PKN dan Rekomendasi Muktamar XIII, lines 104–158 and 308–330](https://id.scribd.com/document/798676224/Pkn-Dan-Rekomendasi-Muktamar-Kammi-Xiii-2024).

The draft's 32-PW table and the Muktamar plan's 34-PW goal show why the August 2024 table cannot be silently treated as current. A KAMMI-hosted September 2025 publication still used the name **KAMMI Kaltimtara**, leaving the later status of the planned Kaltara separation unclear. Source: [KAMMI Kaltimtara publication, 15 September 2025](https://kaltimtara.kammi.id/publikasi/berita/625-PENGURUS-WILAYAH-KAMMI-SE-INDONESIA-TEGASKAN-DUKUNGAN-PADA-KEPEMIMPINAN-SAH-DAN-KECAM-UPAYA-PEMBEGALAN-ORGANISASI).

## Public-data gaps

- The current PP KAMMI homepage contains a “PW/PD KAMMI se-Indonesia” section but currently displays zero counts and no usable unit list: [KAMMI.id](https://www.kammi.id/).
- The homepage's network link points to `https://www.kammi.id/wilayah-daerah`, which returned HTTP 404 on 13 August 2026.
- The current official sitemap lists only the homepage, news, events, about, and central-management pages; it does not list a network registry: [KAMMI sitemap](https://www.kammi.id/sitemap.xml).
- No public first-party source was found that confirms every current PD-to-PW mapping.
- Historic/template counters claiming 176 PW and 955 PD conflict with the Muktamar plan and are not credible as unit counts; they should not seed a taxonomy.
- The organizational documents establish formal categories and hierarchy, but they do not supply survey-ready answers for “never served,” “formerly served,” concurrent service at several levels, unknown membership level, or refusal to answer. Those remain product-policy questions.

## Facts safe to carry into the decision ticket

- Formal ordinary-membership levels are AB I, AB II, and AB III.
- Anggota Kehormatan is a separate membership category.
- Formal management structures are PP, PW, PD, PLN, and PK.
- “Registered at” can refer to an organizational unit rather than domicile.
- PW-to-province and PD-to-city mappings are not necessarily one-to-one.
- A current PW/PD answer set cannot be claimed authoritative from public PP KAMMI data alone; it needs organizational validation or an explicitly versioned provisional source.
